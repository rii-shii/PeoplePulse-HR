# PeoplePulse-HR — Project Findings

## Dataset Overview
- Total employees: 1,470
- Total columns: 35
- Employees who left: 237
- Employees who stayed: 1,233

## Key Metrics
- Attrition rate: 16.12%
- Employee retention rate: 83.88%

## Analysis Performed
- Overall attrition distribution
- Attrition by department
- Attrition by overtime status
- Attrition by job role
- Comparison of age, monthly income, and years at company by attrition status

## Business Recommendations
1. Investigate differences in attrition across departments and job roles.
2. Review overtime patterns and employee workload.
3. Compare tenure and compensation patterns for employees who leave and stay.
4. Use these findings to guide further HR investigation; associations do not establish causation.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and business-oriented interpretation using Python.
